# AnyTogether

**Play the PS5 games you own on your Mac, your PC and your phone, with your friends.**
Open source, made by players.

[Website](https://anytogether.kodla.agency) · [Full specs](https://anytogether.kodla.agency/specs) · [Download](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyPS5-ARM/releases/latest) · [Roadmap](https://anytogether.kodla.agency/roadmap)

### What's here

| Project | What it is | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| **[AnyPS5-ARM](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyPS5-ARM)** | The engine port for Apple silicon and the console-style Mac launcher; every release carries all platforms | macOS |
| **[AnyPS5-Remote](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyPS5-Remote)** | The phone app: your phone as a DualSense, plus friends, party, voice and messages | iOS · Android |
| **[AnyPS5-Desktop](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyPS5-Desktop)** | The launcher for Windows and Linux, with Steam and Steam Deck support | Windows · Linux |
| **[AnyTogether](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyTogether)** | The online side: accounts, friends, parties and voice, messages, trophies, Play Together, Captures, cloud saves. You can run your own server | Web · self-hosted |
| **[Website](https://anytogether.kodla.agency)** | Downloads, full specs, roadmap and your AnyTogether account in the browser. Its code is `apps/web` in the AnyTogether repo | Web |

### Highlights

- 🎮 **Couch-ready launcher**: pad-first navigation, PS quick menu, profiles, controller management (DualSense, DualShock 4/3, Xbox, Switch Pro and more)
- 🌐 **Online play**: a virtual LAN between players over WebRTC, Play Together from a party, Windows, Linux and Mac in the same game
- 📱 **Phone as a controller**: the app, or no app at all, by scanning a QR code in the browser
- 📸 **Captures**: clips and screenshots from the Create button, shared with friends, with likes and comments
- 🔏 **Signed releases**: every file is checked before the launcher installs it

### Get started

1. Download the latest release for your platform: [Mac, phone, Windows and Linux](https://github.com/AnyTogether/AnyPS5-ARM/releases/latest).
2. Add your own decrypted game dumps.
3. Sign in to AnyTogether to play with friends, or stay offline.
